# Reflection

The experiment showed a clear improvement in AI output quality as the prompt strategy became more specific and structured.

The **Zero-shot prompt** produced a relevant 7-day itinerary and covered the main daily requirements, including morning, afternoon, and evening activities. However, its output was presented as a dense list, which made it less organized and harder to scan. As a result, it received a score of **17/25**.

The **Few-shot prompt** improved the structure and consistency of the generated itinerary. By providing an example of the desired format, the AI was able to produce a well-organized Markdown table with clearer categories such as activities, cultural focus, and local food. However, some logistical and budget details were missing. It therefore achieved a score of **19/25**.

The **Refined prompt** produced the strongest result, receiving **25/25**. It provided more explicit instructions about the traveler profile, daily structure, cultural focus, culinary experiences, transportation, and budget considerations. The output was highly structured and closely followed the required constraints, making it more practical and actionable.

Overall, the experiment demonstrates that **prompt design has a significant impact on generative AI output quality**. Moving from Zero-shot to Few-shot improved formatting and consistency, while the Refined prompt further improved completeness, structure, constraint adherence, and usefulness.

The Refined prompt achieved the best result because it combined clear instructions, contextual information, structured output requirements, and specific constraints. The main strength of the experiment was the progressive improvement in organization and usefulness. The main weakness was that more detailed prompting resulted in a more rigid output structure, requiring more careful prompt design.

Based on the actual evaluation results, the **Refined Prompt was the most effective strategy**, achieving the highest score of **25/25**.
