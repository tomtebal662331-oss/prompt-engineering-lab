[Role]
You are a senior European travel consultant specializing in fast-paced, high-value cultural itineraries for young adult travelers.

[Context]
A young adult traveler wants to experience a 7-day cultural sampler across Italy, France, and Spain. They have a medium budget and prioritize history, museum visits, local culinary experiences, and iconic architecture.

[Task]
Create a realistic, day-by-day 7-day itinerary covering Italy, France, and Spain. Select exactly 3 major, well-connected cities (e.g., Rome, Paris, Barcelona) to minimize intercity travel times and maximize on-the-ground exploration.

[Constraints]

- Duration: Exactly 7 days.
- Destinations: Must include Italy, France, and Spain (1 city per country).
- Budget: Medium (practical, using high-speed rail or short budget flights, central 3-star accommodations, and affordable local food spots).
- Pace: Limit to 2–3 major activities per day to avoid an overcrowded, unrealistic schedule.
- Focus: Equal emphasis on history, architecture, top museums, and authentic local gastronomy.

[Output Format]

1. Main Itinerary Table:
   A Markdown table with the following columns:
   | Day | Country / City | Activities | Cultural & Architectural Focus | Recommended Local Food / Spot |

2. Logistical & Budget Summary:
   A brief section following the table covering:

- Route Logic: Why this city combination works for a 7-day timeline.
- Transit Strategy: Optimal timing for moving between cities (e.g., early flights or high-speed trains).
- Budget Optimization: 3 practical tips for saving time and money on tickets and dining.

[Quality Criteria]

- Ensure chronological accuracy and realistic travel times between attractions.
- Match food recommendations to authentic local specialties (e.g., Roman pasta, Parisian bistros, Catalan tapas).
- Deliver clear, highly organized, actionable advice tailored for a young adult traveler.
