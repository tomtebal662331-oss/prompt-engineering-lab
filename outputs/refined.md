## Logistical & Budget Summary

### Route Logic
This Rome–Paris–Barcelona triangle is the ultimate 7-day sampler because all three cities serve as major European transit hubs. Direct, high-frequency budget flights connect them in under 2 hours each, minimizing downtime and leaving maximum time for on-the-ground cultural immersion.

### Transit Strategy
* **Rome to Paris (Day 3):** Book an early morning flight (around 7:00 AM – 8:30 AM) out of Rome Fiumicino (FCO) to Paris Orly (ORY) or CDG to land by mid-morning and utilize the full afternoon.
* **Paris to Barcelona (Day 5):** Take an early flight out of Paris to Barcelona El Prat (BCN) to arrive in Spain before lunch, giving you a full afternoon for Gaudí’s architectural highlights.

### Budget Optimization
1. **Book Skip-the-Line Tickets Early:** Pre-book timed entry tickets for the Colosseum, Louvre, and Sagrada Família at least 3–4 weeks in advance to save hours of queueing and secure standard ticket prices.
2. **City Transit Passes:** Grab a transport pass in Paris (Navigo Easy/Mobilis) and Barcelona (Hola Barcelona) to save on individual metro fares between major sites.
3. **Food Strategy (Prix-Fixe & Tapas):** Eat your main heavy meal at lunch using French Menu du Jour (fixed-price lunch menus) or Italian Pranzo Lavoro, then enjoy casual, affordable tapas in Barcelona for dinner.
