# 7-Day Cultural Trip Itinerary: Italy, France, and Spain

## Day-by-Day Itinerary

* **Day 1 (Rome - Ancient Wonders):** Morning visit to the Colosseum & Roman Forum (pre-booked ticket). Lunch: authentic Carbonara in Monti. Afternoon at the Capitoline Museums. Evening walk around the Pantheon and Trevi Fountain.
* **Day 2 (Rome - Vatican & Art):** Morning exploration of the Vatican Museums & Sistine Chapel. Afternoon visit to St. Peter's Basilica. Evening walk through Trastevere with local street food (Supplì).
* **Day 3 (Transit to Paris & Iconic Landmarks):** Morning flight from Rome to Paris. Check-in and afternoon walk around the Eiffel Tower & Champ de Mars. Evening bistro dinner in Le Marais.
* **Day 4 (Paris - Masterpieces & Architecture):** Morning visit to the Louvre Museum. Afternoon walk through Tuileries Garden and Sainte-Chapelle for Gothic stained glass. Evening pastry tasting along the Seine.
* **Day 5 (Paris - Bohemian Vibe & Transit):** Morning exploration of Montmartre & Sacré-Cœur Basilica. Afternoon flight or high-speed train to Barcelona. Evening tapas tour in the Gothic Quarter.
* **Day 6 (Barcelona - Modernist Architecture):** Morning visit to Sagrada Família (pre-booked ticket). Lunch at La Boqueria Market. Afternoon stroll through Park Güell to admire Gaudí's architecture.
* **Day 7 (Barcelona - Historic & Coastal Vibe):** Morning walk through the El Born district and Picasso Museum. Afternoon relaxing along Barceloneta Beach. Evening farewell paella dinner by the sea.
